How I Work

I believe analytics should go beyond answering questions — it should create clarity, simplify complex processes, and help people make better decisions.

My approach combines engineering discipline, business understanding, and data-driven thinking to design solutions that are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with real operational needs.

I focus on understanding the context behind the data: how systems work, how processes impact the business, and how information can be transformed into meaningful insights for different stakeholders.

I am especially interested in enterprise analytics, ERP ecosystems, business intelligence solutions, cloud-based data environments, and workflow automation.
